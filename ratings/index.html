Reed’s Leans

Team totals Testing

Each team's points from RKR (RKR total and RKR line) against the team total the market implies from its total and spread. Backtest, 2021–26 (walk-forward): team-total unders 4+ points under the opening number went 88-53 (62.4%), 3–4 points 54.7%, but only 52% in 2025–26; against the closing number they were a coin flip (49–51%), and overs never showed an edge. So Strong = under 4+ vs the opener with the opening number still available; Lean = 3+; everything else is Watch. Testing only, tracked separately from the best-bet record. Books post their own team totals: check your number, the edge moves 1 for 1.

Reed’s Parlays

Built from this week's index picks. Every leg uses the hit rate its pick type earned in the 2021–26 backtest (★★★ 57%, ★★ 54%, ★ 48–50%). A parlay is only +EV if its legs are: good legs multiply the edge, weak legs multiply the loss. Each card shows the hit chance, the fair odds (what it's really worth) and the payout at standard −110 prices. Only bet a parlay if your book pays at least the fair odds.

Reed’s Same-Game Parlays

One per game, from that matchup's model legs: the side, game-total and team-total unders, and the player props the model rates Strong or Lean, priced together in 20,000 simulated games (player stats are linked to their team's and the opponent's scoring with weights fitted on 2025–26 game logs). Tap a game to see its parlays. Each one is logged before kickoff and graded when the game goes final. Only bet one if your book's same-game price is longer than the fair odds.

Player prop parlays

Build your own prop parlay

Tick any of the model's prop plays. Payout uses each leg's posted price; the hit chance treats legs as independent, so legs from the same game (especially a QB and his own receiver) are correlated and the book will price that combo itself as a same-game parlay.

Legs are the model's Strong and Lean prop plays against posted book lines (injury-flagged legs are left out of the ready-made parlays). Hit chance uses the model's blended probability for each leg (40% model, 60% market). Fair odds is what the parlay is really worth; it's only +EV if your book pays more. Parlays are high-variance: even good ones lose most of the time, so keep stakes small (0.1–0.25u). For entertainment only. 21+. 1-800-GAMBLER.

Live betting

Type in the score, clock and your book's live line. RKR updates its pregame number for the score and time left, starts from the pregame closing line (frozen at kickoff) plus 30% of RKR's disagreement on the side and 15% on the total, adjusts for the score, the time left and who has the ball using patterns from 6,400+ games drive by drive (scoring pace so far barely predicts the rest; big leads shrink a little; scoring is back-loaded; totals have a long upper tail, so overs are watch-only), and tells you whether the live number is worth it. Momentum by itself isn't a reason to bet: the calls look for spots where the live line has moved further than the game state justifies.

Live lines & line movement

How the model measures a moving line
  • The number: every ~10 minutes the current spread and total are pulled from 10 sportsbooks (DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, Caesars, Fanatics, ESPN BET, Hard Rock, BetRivers, Bovada, Courtside). The market line is the median; the bet is graded at the best number available.
  • Same model, same rules: the RKR index line doesn't change with the market (it moves only with injuries and the weekly rebuild). Each update re-runs the v3.1 rules on the new number: power-conference edge 4+ = Best Bet, Group of 5 edge 4–6, edge 3–4 = Lean; totals: unders 5+ below the line = Best Bet.
  • Line moves: movement is measured from the opener. A play that only qualifies after the line moved is marked "after line move" (those went about .500 in testing, so they're weaker). A move of 3+ points against RKR turns a play into a Lean with a news check.
  • Toward vs away: "toward RKR" means the market has moved closer to RKR line since open (the money agrees with the model; good for closing-line value). "Away" means the market is moving against the index.
  • Status changes below are logged with the time, so you can see exactly when a play appeared or dropped.

Reed’s Heisman Watch

Live odds + RKR production.

Loading…

Heisman % = 85% the live betting market (DraftKings odds via ESPN, with the bookmaker's margin removed) + 15% the RKR production score: season value added (total PPA on passes, runs and catches, garbage time excluded) × a team-success factor (0.6 + 0.6 × win %) × 0.8 outside the Power 4. Voters follow the narrative the market prices, so odds carry most of the weight; production flags players the market may be under- or over-rating (▲/▼ vs odds). Odds refresh every 15 minutes; production updates weekly. For entertainment only.

Reed's Computers (BCS-style)

Four computer formulas modeled on the BCS computers, which were barred from using margin of victory after 2001: they count only who you beat, who you lost to, and how good those teams are. Colley = Colley Matrix. Wolfe = win-probability rating. Elo-chess = Sagarin's win/loss Elo. A&H = record + schedule, Anderson & Hester-style. Like the BCS, each team's best and worst computer ranks are dropped and the middle two are averaged; Comp % = points from those two ranks (25 for 1st … 1 for 25th) out of 50. This is a résumé ranking: it feeds Reed's committee rank and bracketology (half of the résumé score), not the betting lines. Power = rank in the RKR Power Ratings, for comparison.
